Blackgrid on Ember

Static mock-up, not the app. Item names, prices, needs and the map are illustrative. Colours, type, radii and controls come from brand/tokens.css, brand/components.css and DESIGN.md's Blackgrid extensions. The map is abstract geometry standing in for the real artwork. B is the direction you picked; A and C are here only to compare. B, round 2: real marker icons with switches, map picker, raid timers in the top bar, pictures in history, labelled Settings and a full Settings page. Marker switches, the map picker, Settings and the status bar items are clickable.
