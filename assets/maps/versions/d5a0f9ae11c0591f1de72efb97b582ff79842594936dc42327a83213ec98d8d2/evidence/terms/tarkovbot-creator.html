      Welcome to TarkovBOT

      Discord Bot

      Our Discord bot offers a wealth of information to your server, including current item prices, the real-time status of EFT servers, the current Tarkov time, and traders' restock times. You can even use the bot to locate the probable location of the Goons thanks to our tracker. And when you're feeling bored, generate some excitement by using the 'randomgear' command to create unique gear combinations.

      More Info Invite to Discord

      Twitch Bot

      Our Twitch bot offers a range of helpful commands to enhance your streaming experience. E.g 'price' command allows you to check prices on the fly, just like our Discord bot or you can search for things on the wiki without having to search the wiki manually, you get a link straight from the bot. There is also 'randomgear' command to generate unique gear combinations. It's a great tool to engage your audience and create interactive streams.

      Goons Tracker

      Introducing our Goons Tracker! With our tracker, you and your fellow mates can easily report the Goons or get their location through community reports. By leveraging these reports, you can swiftly check the current location. Enhance your hunting experience and save your time. You can also use our overlays, twitch bot or discord bot to report The Goons location as well. We would be happy if you would help us and the community by reporting their location.
